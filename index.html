The problem

Threat hunting still runs on copy and paste

Most people — and most investigators — check a suspicious email the same way they did five years ago: manually, one tool at a time, after the fact.

Copy-paste triage

Investigators paste the same link or IP into four or five different lookup tools before drawing a conclusion.

Invisible spoofing

Lookalike domains and forged sender headers pass a casual glance — the difference is a single swapped character.

No forensic trail

Once a phishing email is deleted, the indicators it contained usually disappear with it.

Reactive, not real-time

Detection usually happens after someone has already clicked — reporting is a post-mortem, not a safeguard.

What Sentryl does

One scan. Every indicator checked.

Sentryl runs the checks an investigator would — automatically, inline, the moment an email is opened.

URL

URL reputation

Links are cross-checked against live threat-intelligence feeds the instant they render on screen.

IP / HEADER

IP & header forensics

Received-path IPs and SPF, DKIM, and DMARC results are parsed automatically from raw headers.

DOMAIN

Spoof detection

Flags lookalike domains and display-name mismatches that don't match the sender's real address.

SCORE

Risk scoring

Every check rolls up into one explainable score, instead of five disconnected results to interpret yourself.

INLINE

Inline alerts

Warnings appear next to the email itself — no separate dashboard or tab to keep checking.

EXPORT

Forensic reports

Generate a one-click summary of every indicator found, ready to hand to an investigator or IT team.

How it works

From inbox to verdict in under a second

Sentryl runs entirely alongside the email client you already use — no separate app to open.

01

Read

Sentryl parses the email's DOM and headers the moment it loads in Gmail or Outlook.

02

Extract

URLs, IPs, domains, and sender fields are pulled out — nothing else is read from the message.

03

Check

Each indicator is compared against threat-intelligence sources and authentication records.

04

Score

A risk badge appears inline, with a full breakdown available on demand.

Permissions, in plain terms

Sentryl only sees what it needs to

No account access, no silent data collection. Here's exactly what that means.

✓ What Sentryl reads

  • Links, sender headers, and IPs in the email you currently have open
  • Publicly available threat-intelligence data, for comparison only
  • Authentication results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) already present in the message

✕ What Sentryl never touches

  • Full email body content — only extracted indicators are processed, never stored
  • Contacts, attachment contents, or account credentials
  • Any message other than the one you currently have open
